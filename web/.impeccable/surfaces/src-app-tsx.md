## Scope

Whole web app (found-items home, item detail + claim, report found, report lost, sign in, guard panel). Visitor mode: Operate. Mobile-first, phone used on campus; guard panel at the booth.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every found item is a notice pinned to a Thai faculty board, and its fate is stamped on it. Refuses the white-card marketplace grid with a purple gradient brand.

OWN-WORLD: Felt-board green shell (header, footer, page ground behind notices); off-white A4 paper surfaces; Thai card-stock colours (yellow, pink, sky, mint, orange, lilac) as category and route hues at full strength; red push-pins and red-ink stamps as status; Itim marker voice for page titles only, Anuphan for everything else. Scalloped yellow trim edges the header. No gradients, no glass, no glow.

STORY: The owner sees the board, searches or taps a category, recognises their item by its card-stock header and building code, opens it, answers the verification question, and goes to the named booth.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Felt header with BU mark, language and theme, marker title, one-line lead, full-width paper search field; scalloped trim; category chips in card-stock colours; two-up pinned notices (card-stock header with category and building code, photo, title, place and time, status stamp); paper bottom tab bar with mint report-found button.

FORM: Faculty notice board, my grounded list position 6 of 7. seed:5c7a5873132f45da. Signature move: status stamps (round 'waiting', rectangle 'verified', struck 'returned') — never colour alone. Raises: labelled segments on item detail (boarding pass), building code links to place filter (doujin catalog), one card-stock hue per top-level route (acetate manual).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
